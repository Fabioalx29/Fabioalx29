### 👋 About me

I'm a high school senior who fell in love with turning static pages into things people actually enjoy clicking through. No computer science degree yet, no fancy job title — just a habit of shipping a new project every time I learn something new.

In the last few months I've built a burger shop with a real shopping cart, an animated login screen, and an interactive travel page — each one an excuse to get better at layout, motion, and writing cleaner code than the last time.

**Right now I'm looking for my first internship or junior front-end role** — a team willing to trade some mentorship for someone who shows up curious and ships fast.
